Biography

-Donghwan Lee

Donghwan Lee is an Associate Professor in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST and an adjunct faculty member in the KAIST Robotics Program. His research develops reliable reinforcement-learning and learning-based control methods with mathematical guarantees, with applications to autonomous and industrial systems.

He received a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Konkuk University, an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from Yonsei University, and an M.S. in Mathematics and a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Purdue University. From 2017 to 2020, he was a postdoctoral researcher in the Coordinated Science Laboratory at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
