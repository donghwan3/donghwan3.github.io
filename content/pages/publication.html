  • A Discrete-Time Switching System Analysis of Q-Learning, SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization, 2023 [arxiv] [journal] [video]

Donghwan Lee, Jianghai Hu, and Niao He

Models asynchronous Q-learning with a constant step size as a stochastic discrete-time affine switching system, deriving finite-time error bounds through upper and lower comparison systems and providing new insight into Q-learning’s overestimation bias.

  • New Versions of Gradient Temporal-Difference Learning, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 2023 [arxiv] [journal] [video]

Donghwan Lee, Han-Dong Lim, Jihoon Park, and Okyong Choi

Develops new variants of gradient temporal-difference learning within a unified convex–concave saddle-point framework, providing systematic stability analysis for off-policy learning with linear function approximation.

  • Convergence of Dynamic Programming on the Semidefinite Cone for Discrete-Time Infinite-Horizon LQR, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 2022 [arxiv] [journal] [video]

Donghwan Lee

Establishes matrix-inequality and norm-based convergence guarantees for dynamic programming in discrete-time infinite-horizon LQR, proving exponential convergence of Q-value iteration under a mild initialization condition and showing that policy iteration always converges exponentially fast.

  • A Unified Switching System Perspective and Convergence Analysis of Q-Learning Algorithms, NeurIPS 2020 [arxiv] [conference] [video]

Donghwan Lee and Niao He

Models a broad family of Q-learning algorithms as switching systems and develops convergence results

  • Target-Based Temporal-Difference Learning, ICML 2019 [arxiv] [conference] [video]

Donghwan Lee and Niao He 

Provides a theoretical foundation for target-network-style updates in temporal-difference learning.

  • Help Me Through: Imitation Learning Based Active View Planning to Avoid SLAM Tracking Failures, IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2025 [journal]

Kanwal Naveed, Wajahat Hussain, Irfan Hussain, Donghwan Lee, Muhammad Latif Anjum

  • Power Consumption Optimization of GPU Server With Offline Reinforcement Learning, IEEE Access 2025 [journal]
