  • A discrete-time switching system analysis of Q-learning, SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization, 2023 [link]

Donghwan Lee, Jianghai Hu, and Niao He

Models asynchronous Q-learning with a constant step size as a stochastic discrete-time affine switching system, deriving finite-time error bounds through upper and lower comparison systems and providing new insight into Q-learning’s overestimation bias.

  • New versions of gradient temporal-difference learning, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 2023 [link]

Donghwan Lee, Han-Dong Lim, Jihoon Park, and Okyong Choi

Develops new variants of gradient temporal-difference learning within a unified convex–concave saddle-point framework, providing systematic stability analysis for off-policy learning with linear function approximation.

  • Convergence of dynamic programming on the semidefininte cone for discrete-time infinite-horizon LQR, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 2022 [link]

Donghwan Lee

Establishes matrix-inequality and norm-based convergence guarantees for dynamic programming in discrete-time infinite-horizon LQR, proving exponential convergence of Q-value iteration under a mild initialization condition and showing that policy iteration always converges exponentially fast.

  • A unified switching system perspective and convergence analysis of Q-learning algorithms, NeurIPS2020 [link]

Donghwan Lee and Niao He

Models a broad family of Q-learning algorithms as switching systems and develops convergence results

  • Target-based temporal difference learning, ICML2019 [link]

Donghwan Lee and Niao He
