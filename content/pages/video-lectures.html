About the book

The companion textbook builds the mathematical framework for sequential decision-making, from Markov decision processes and value functions to Bellman equations, dynamic programming, and learning from sampled experience. Read the relevant sections alongside the videos to follow the notation, revisit the proofs, and work through the examples.

Topics covered

  • Markov decision processes: states, actions, transitions, policies, rewards, and discounted returns.
  • Value functions and Bellman equations: state and action values, advantage functions, contraction mappings, and optimality.
  • Dynamic programming: value iteration, stopping rules, linear programming, and occupancy measures.
  • Mathematical tools: gradient methods, regression, entropy, KL divergence, and softmax.
  • Learning from experience: stochastic approximation, Monte Carlo prediction, and exploration.
