Abstract

A summary of the paper, written for this page.

The paper augments the state of a continuous-time T-S fuzzy system with membership-weighted and quadratic terms. A quadratic function of this lifted state becomes a richer Lyapunov function of the original state. Structural identities and slack variables recover information lost by a naive augmentation.

The problem

Why this problem matters

Ignoring the dependence of membership functions on state can make stability tests conservative. Existing fuzzy Lyapunov tests may also require bounds on membership-function time derivatives.

Assumptions and scope

The result requires differentiable membership functions and the stated representation of their derivatives. It avoids prescribed derivative bounds, not all derivative information. The tests are sufficient and local; augmentation increases computational cost.
