Abstract

A summary of the paper, written for this page.

Periodic Q-learning separates online and target Q-tables. With a frozen target, the inner loop becomes stochastic optimization of a quadratic loss. The outer loop approximates a Bellman contraction, making optimization error and target-update error separately visible in a finite-sample analysis.

The problem

Why this problem matters

Target networks are common in deep RL, but their analysis is difficult. The tabular setting isolates periodic target updates from the additional effects of nonlinear approximation.

From problem to solution

How the method works

Optimize with a fixed target

The inner objective is strongly convex in a tabular Q vector when every state-action pair has positive sampling probability. Its solution is the Bellman update of the frozen target.

Algorithm 1 and inner objective

Account for inexact Bellman updates

Each inner optimization leaves an error. The outer contraction discounts earlier errors, while the initial-condition contribution decays geometrically.

Reading the guarantees

Assumptions and scope

The results concern finite discounted MDPs under the paper’s independent sampling model and specified inner-loop budgets. They provide a tabular explanation of target updates, not a convergence proof for deep Q-networks or a claim of current minimax optimality.
