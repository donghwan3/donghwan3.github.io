Abstract

A summary of the paper, written for this page.

Log-sum-exp and Boltzmann backups smooth the maximum in different ways. This paper places both algorithms between comparison systems and bounds their finite-time error relative to the ordinary optimal Q-function. The bounds retain distinct step-size and temperature-dependent residuals.

The problem

Why this problem matters

Smoothing a Bellman backup changes its target as well as its dynamics. A convergence statement must identify both the reference value and the error introduced by smoothing.

From problem to solution

How the method works

Bound each soft operator

Log-sum-exp lies above the maximum; the Boltzmann weighted average lies below it. Both gaps are controlled by log of the action count divided by inverse temperature.

Assumptions and scope

The analysis assumes tabular discounted learning, independent sampling with positive visitation probabilities, bounded rewards and initialization, and constant step size in (0,1). It does not claim exact convergence to the ordinary optimum at fixed temperature and step size.
