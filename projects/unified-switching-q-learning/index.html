Abstract

A summary of the paper, written for this page.

The nonlinear mean dynamics of Q-learning can be organized as a switched affine system. Comparison principles establish stability and support asymptotic convergence proofs. The framework covers asynchronous and averaging Q-learning, with a restricted extension to linear function approximation.

The problem

Why this problem matters

Changing greedy policies create nonlinear mean dynamics. The aim is to analyze those dynamics through switching theory without assuming one fixed policy throughout learning.
